President's Message

Message from the President

Welcome to the official website of ONGC Ex-Employees Association (OEWA), Kerala.

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome every retired ONGC colleague and their family members to our Association. Since its formation in 2019, OEWA Kerala has worked to strengthen friendship, unity, promote welfare activities and provide a common platform for mutual support to retired employees across the State.

This website also to provides easy access to notices, committee information, membership details, subscriptions, financial statements, and important announcements.

We encourage every member to actively participate in our activities and make use of this portal for staying connected with the association to help us make our Association stronger and more vibrant. Together we can continue to serve our members and uphold the values that have guided us throughout our careers.

Thank you for your continued support and cooperation.

Warm Regards,

President
