About Our Association

Who We Are

ONGC Ex-Employees Association (OEWA), Kerala was established in 2019 with the objective of bringing together retired employees of Oil and Natural Gas Corporation (ONGC) residing in Kerala. The Association serves as a common platform for fostering friendship, mutual support, and continued engagement among former colleagues.

OEWA strives to protect the interests of retired ONGC employees by promoting welfare activities, disseminating useful information, organising meetings, and maintaining close coordination with ONGC and other organisations whenever required.

Objectives

  • Promote fellowship among retired ONGC employees.
  • Safeguard members' welfare and common interests.
  • Organise meetings, seminars and social gatherings.
  • Disseminate information on pension, medical benefits and welfare schemes.
  • Encourage cultural, educational and charitable activities.
  • Maintain cordial relations with ONGC and similar organisations.

Vision

To build a vibrant and caring community of retired ONGC employees where every member remains connected, respected and supported.
